THE PROCESS

Four stages.
One annual celebration.

01 / FAN VOTE

Open nominations

Fans vote for entries they believe deserve recognition in each of the nine award categories.

02 / THE NOMINEES

Top twenty

The 20 highest-supported eligible entries in each category are announced as nominees.

03 / FINAL DROP

Final fan vote

Fans vote again from the official nominee list to choose the award recipients.

04 / SEPTEMBER 30

Shimei Awards

The yearly winners are announced on September 30 and preserved in Archionary.
